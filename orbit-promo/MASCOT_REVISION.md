# ORBIT mascot revision

The round character has been replaced in all four mascot scenes by a new open-book study companion: ivory pages, teal covers, coral bookmark, navy shoes, stethoscope and a tilted orbit ring. The brief was newly written and the artwork generated for this revision.

The old derivative bot engine is removed from this source. Remotion motion now uses deterministic gentle floating and rocking. Narration, music, screenshots other than the avatar, university coverage and timing are unchanged.

Both Ask AI screenshots were recaptured from an isolated source preview with the new image in the avatar component. This is a proposed design preview; the installed app and GitHub app repository have not been changed. The preview alias and capture script are included for reproduction.

The reusable transparent artwork is `public/orbit-study-mascot.png`. Changing the design reduces its visual similarity to the old round mascot, but does not establish copyright or trademark clearance. No comprehensive legal clearance search was performed.
