The film labels these as **listed MBBS colleges**, and says **Not ORBIT users**. Neither document establishes ORBIT adoption, student recommendations, institutional endorsement, or complete compatibility of every historical college exam paper. No testimonial or recommendation count is fabricated.

The counts describe the affiliated university audience for the two existing question-bank patterns. Admission permissions and affiliation can change, so update the dated sources before reusing the count in a later campaign.
