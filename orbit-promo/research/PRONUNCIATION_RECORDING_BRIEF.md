## Findings

The previous checks compared text metadata and clip timings. Those checks cannot establish how spoken words sound. Short isolated clips, spaced letters such as “A C E V” and “M C Qs”, repeated full stops and globally slowed speech are candidate causes of awkward delivery. The user reports pronunciation errors; no blind human listening evaluation was performed.

Ramp’s confirmed launch/product workflow uses ElevenLabs, but the published customer story does not name the exact voice preset. Do not claim a preset identified merely from listening. Choose and audition a licensed American English commercial voice in the current library, then compare it using the actual ORBIT script.

## Recording direction

Warm, clear American English. Conversational confidence, restrained energy, natural cadence. Use connected phrases rather than announcer-style isolated syllables. Record at the voice’s native pace, then shorten wording if it does not fit. Do not force timing by stretching or accelerating speech. Test the dry recording before mixing.
